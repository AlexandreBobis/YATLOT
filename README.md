# You Are The Last Of Them

*Read this in [Français](README.fr.md).*

You Are The Last Of Them (YATLOT) is a Dev Game made with Inky, the official Inkle programming language. Enjoy !

## Story

### Beginning 

It is a story about a guy that woke up in a strange place, an island and he doesn't remember anything. He has to find out to survive and how to get out of there. The island is full of mysteries and he has to solve them to get out of there.

### Middle

There are different places to explore. You can fish, collect stickwoods, and stones. You can also find a cave, a tent, and pieces of puzzle. You have to make choices to survive and to get out of there.

### End

There are different endings in the game. It depends on the choices you make:

- Find out the pieces of the puzzle
- No longer having resources

#### Pieces of the puzzle

There are four pieces of the puzzle to find. You can find them in different places:

- By fishing
- By collecting stones (you will find a cave with the piece of the puzzle)
- By exchanging it with a monkey (against stickwoods)
- By finding it in the tent (free the parrot)

I uses random numbers, you have 1% of chance to find one in fishing and collecting stones.

## Installation

Clone this repository and open ./YouAreTheLastOfThem/index.html

## Technology

YATLOT is made with [Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/), the official Inkle programming language. Inky is a simple way to learn programming and to create interactive stories.
